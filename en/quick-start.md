# Your First Recording

> **Status: Review draft; content pending verification.** Resolve all “To confirm” items before publishing as user instructions.

[Guide home](index.md) · [简体中文](../zh/quick-start.md)

Goal: record a short test, export the data, and check that it is usable.

## Before you begin

Prepare the device, the power and storage accessories specified for your kit, and a computer for checking the data. Run your first test in a safe environment with stable lighting.

> **To confirm:** The final packing list, charging requirements, supported storage media, and the exact controls for each step below must be verified against the shipped device. This page does not yet replace the device operating instructions.

## 1. Prepare the device

Check the device and cables for damage. Make sure the lenses are clean, power is sufficient, and storage space is available. Locate the serial number (SN) so you can identify the data or request support later.

## 2. Fit the device and check the view

Adjust the fit so the device stays secure without blocking your vision. Check each camera view for hair, clothing, or accessories covering the lens. Keep the recording target within the usable field of view.

> **To confirm:** A fitting illustration, adjustment instructions, and the preview entry point.

## 3. Record a short test

Start recording using the controls supplied with your device version. Confirm that recording has started, then perform a simple task. Stop recording when finished and wait for saving to complete before removing storage or disconnecting the device.

> **To confirm:** Power-on, start, and stop controls; indicators for recording, saving, and save completion. Do not infer button timings or indicator meanings from this page.

## 4. Export and check the data

Use a transfer method supported by your device to copy the complete session folder to your computer. Keep its original structure; copying only the videos is insufficient. Check for missing files and play sample videos to verify that the task was captured.

**Success looks like:** You can locate the session, the file check reports no missing files, and the test footage clearly captures the intended actions. See [Export & Verify](export.md) for details.

## 5. Upload to Collecta if needed

Make sure the device is registered and assigned to the correct collector. Select the complete session folder in the Collecta uploader, pass the file check, and upload. Then check processing and quality results on the platform. Raw recording files are uploaded through the uploader rather than the website.

Before a full recording, review the [recording checklist](recording.md).
