# Recording Guide

> **Status: Review draft; content pending verification.** Resolve all “To confirm” items before publishing as user instructions.

[Guide home](index.md) · [简体中文](../zh/recording.md)

Goal: complete essential checks before each recording and finish with complete, verifiable data.

## Before recording

- [ ] Lenses are clean and every camera view is unobstructed.
- [ ] The device fits securely and comfortably without restricting vision or movement.
- [ ] Battery and available storage are sufficient for the task.
- [ ] The task, location, and actions to capture are clear.
- [ ] Device time has been checked as required for the task; time issues have been resolved.
- [ ] A test recording has been checked after first use, setting changes, or accessory changes.

## During recording

1. Start recording and confirm that the device is recording.
2. Perform the task, keeping key actions within the camera views wherever possible.
3. Watch for device warnings. If recording fails, finish the current activity safely and investigate.
4. Stop recording when the task is complete and wait for saving to finish.

> **To confirm:** Recording controls, status indicators, and the actual warnings and recovery steps for low storage, low battery, or interrupted recording. See [Device Overview](device-overview.md).

## After recording

Check that a session was created. Sample the beginning and end of the videos to confirm the task is complete and the views are clear. Copy the complete session folder to your working computer and follow [Export & Verify](export.md).

Do not remove storage, disconnect power, or move original files during recording or saving. Before clearing device storage, confirm that a complete, usable copy exists.

## Reduce repeat recordings

| Easily missed issue | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Key actions move out of view | Run a short test in the same working posture |
| Hair or clothing covers a lens | Check every view after fitting and after adjustments |
| Lighting makes the scene hard to see | Test at the task location and avoid strong backlighting |
| Only video files were copied | Save and upload complete session folders |

For problems, see [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).
