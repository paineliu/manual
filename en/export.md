# Export & Verify

> **Status: Review draft; content pending verification.** Resolve all “To confirm” items before publishing as user instructions.

[Guide home](index.md) · [简体中文](../zh/export.md)

Goal: preserve the complete recording and verify a successful export or upload.

## 1. Export the complete session

1. Stop recording and wait for saving to finish.
2. Connect storage or your computer using a method supported by the shipped device.
3. Locate the session folder and copy the entire folder to the destination.
4. Keep folder structure and filenames unchanged. Do not copy only MP4 files or remove companion files.

> **To confirm:** The data connection method, session location, and safe disconnection procedure for the shipped Ego 4. Do not assume support for direct USB access, wireless downloads, or removable storage.

## 2. Check the copy

- Verify session identity: device SN, session name, and task.
- Compare file counts and sizes with the source, and check for copy errors.
- Play samples from every video stream, including the start, end, and key actions.
- If a validation tool is supplied, check session completeness and review its report.

Playable videos are only an initial check, not proof of full data integrity or quality. See [Files & Data Formats](data-formats.md).

## 3. Upload to Collecta if needed

1. Sign in with an activated account to the Collecta environment specified for your project.
2. Confirm that the device SN is registered, active, and assigned to the correct collector.
3. Download the Collecta uploader for your operating system from the platform's download entry point.
4. Create an upload job, select the complete session folder, and check the device and collector.
5. Select a published task, enter the required task information, and pass the file completeness check before queuing the upload.
6. Wait for upload completion, then open the session on the website to review processing and quality results.

Raw recording files are uploaded through the uploader rather than the website. Keep source files accessible during upload; do not remove storage, close the app, or let the computer sleep. The uploading operator and the collector who owns the data may differ; ownership follows the device assignment.

**Success looks like:** The local copy passes your checks. For Collecta, processing must also finish and quality results must meet project requirements. Review warnings; upload progress alone is insufficient.

## When to clear original data

Confirm that a complete, usable copy exists and that project backup and retention requirements are met before clearing originals through the approved process. An upload-complete message alone does not confirm successful processing.
