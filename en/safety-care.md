## Before use and while wearing

Inspect the device, fittings, and cables for damage. Adjust the fit so vision and movement remain clear. Stop and adjust if wearing the device is uncomfortable. Before recording, confirm that recording is permitted at the location and obtain any authorization required by your project.

## Power and charging

Use the power accessories specified for your kit or confirmed as compatible. Stop using the device and contact support if cables are damaged or the device develops an unusual smell, excessive heat, or deformation.

> **To confirm:** Rated power, charging method, battery requirements, permitted environmental conditions, and other model-specific restrictions. Approved delivery instructions govern the final safety requirements.

## Cleaning and storage

Finish recording and disconnect power as instructed before cleaning. Avoid scratching lenses with hard objects and do not disassemble the device yourself. Follow the approved maintenance instructions for cleaning materials, wearable parts, and storage conditions.

> **To confirm:** Approved lens and housing cleaning methods, removable parts, maintenance intervals, and environmental ranges. Do not assume water resistance or compatibility with alcohol or other cleaning agents.

## Protect your data

Do not remove storage or disconnect power during recording or saving. Keep complete session copies and follow project access, backup, and retention requirements. Back up data as instructed by support before troubleshooting or returning a device for service.
